The Quiet Room

From Black Ridge Case Wiki

The Quiet Room is the informal name given to an unidentified location connected to later investigations of the Elias Voss case.

Archived descriptions repeatedly reference unfinished concrete, fluorescent lighting, plastic covering a window, and a persistent low-frequency room tone.
