Elias Nathaniel Voss is a convicted murderer associated with a series of killings and disappearances in Black Ridge County.

Investigators connected Voss to several unusual cassette recordings recovered from locations associated with the case.

These recordings became collectively known as The Static Tapes.

The Black Ridge investigation

Several locations connected to Voss contained cassette recordings bearing handwritten labels.

The recordings contain long periods of static, environmental noise, and partially intelligible speech.
